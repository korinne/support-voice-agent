# Jet-Powered Pogo Stick safety

The Jet-Powered Pogo Stick is for riders aged 16 and older. Always wear a helmet, ride outdoors on flat ground, and keep at least 3 meters from people, pets, and parked cars.

## Fuel and the thruster

- Let the thruster cool for 10 minutes before refueling.
- Use only Acme Pogo Fuel. Never use gasoline.
- Store the pogo stick upright, away from heat and direct sun.

## If you smell fuel or see a leak

Stop riding right away. Move the pogo stick outdoors, away from anything that can burn, and contact support. Safety reports go to the front of the queue, and a person on the safety team follows up.

## PS-2 valve replacement

Pogo sticks with model number **PS-2** made before March 2026 can develop a slow fuel leak at the valve. The model number is printed under the foot pegs. If you own one, stop using it and contact support for a free replacement valve kit, which ships at no cost.
