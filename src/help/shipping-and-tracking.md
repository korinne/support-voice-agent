# Shipping and tracking

Acme ships every order from our warehouse within one business day of purchase.

## Shipping options

- **Standard:** 5 to 7 business days. Free on orders over $50, otherwise $6.
- **Express:** 2 business days for $12.

You get an email with a tracking link as soon as your order leaves the warehouse. Your order history shows the same link.

## When tracking stops updating

Carriers sometimes skip scans, so a pause of a day or two is normal. If tracking hasn't changed for **3 business days**, contact support and we'll open a trace with the carrier.

If the carrier can't find the package within **7 business days** of the trace, we send a replacement at no cost or refund the order, whichever you prefer.

## Wrong address

We can change the shipping address until the order ships. After that, the carrier controls the package, and we can only ask them to return it to us.
