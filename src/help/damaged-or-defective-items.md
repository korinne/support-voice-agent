# Damaged or defective items

If a gadget arrives damaged or stops working right away, report it within **14 days of delivery**. Tell us the order number and what's wrong. A photo helps, but you can send it later.

## What we can offer

- **A replacement**, shipped free by Express.
- **Store credit** for part or all of the item's price.
- **A refund** after you return the item with a free label.

## How store credit works

The support assistant can request store credit for you on the call, but it can't grant credit itself. A member of the Acme support team reviews every credit request before it's applied. During business hours, most reviews take a few minutes. Outside business hours, expect an answer the next business day.

Credit can't exceed what you paid for the item. Once approved, it appears on your account and applies automatically at your next checkout.
