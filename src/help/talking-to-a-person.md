# Talking to a person

The Acme voice assistant answers questions, looks up your orders, and opens support tickets at any time of day.

## Support team hours

People on the Acme support team work Monday to Friday, 8 AM to 6 PM Pacific time. They review store credit requests and follow up on tickets.

## Getting a person to follow up

Ask the assistant to open a ticket and say that you'd like a person to contact you. Tickets are sorted by topic and urgency, so they reach the right team.

- **Safety issues** are handled first, every day of the week.
- **Shipping and billing** tickets get a reply within one business day.
- **Product questions** get a reply within two business days.
