# Account, payments, and store credit

## Payments

Acme accepts major credit and debit cards. We charge your card when your order ships, not when you place it.

## Store credit

Store credit applies automatically at checkout, before your card is charged. It doesn't expire, and it can't be exchanged for cash.

Credit for a damaged or defective item is requested by support and approved by a member of the support team. Your account page shows each request as pending, approved, or declined.

## Billing questions

If you see a charge you don't recognize, contact support with the date and amount. We can confirm which order it belongs to. For a duplicate charge, we refund the extra payment within 5 business days.
