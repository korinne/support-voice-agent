# Warranty

Every Acme gadget comes with a **one-year warranty** against manufacturing defects. The Anvil-Proof Helmet is covered for **two years**.

## What the warranty covers

- Parts that fail during normal use, such as a spring that won't spring or a hinge that won't fold.
- Electronics that stop working without being dropped, soaked, or opened.

## What it doesn't cover

- Damage from misuse, such as using a Pocket Trampoline as a ladder.
- Normal wear, scratches, and faded paint.
- Gadgets that have been modified or repaired by someone else.

## Making a claim

Contact support with your order number and a short description of the problem. We may ask for a photo or a short video. If the claim is approved, we repair or replace the gadget at no cost, including shipping both ways.
